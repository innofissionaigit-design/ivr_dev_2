1. What this system is

One NVIDIA L4-class GPU runs three ASR checkpoints and three TTS checkpoints resident at once — Bengali, Hindi, English — with the language identified from audio before recognition starts. Exactly one step in a turn is a language model, and it only decides what the caller wants. Every price, date, doctor and confirmation number comes from the clinic database, substituted into fixed wording.

The merge widened the deterministic guard chain (Stage 5) from 3 guards to 11. Every new behaviour therefore reaches the caller before the model runs, which is why intent_schema.py and the LLM prompt could stay byte-identical and the model's accuracy on the existing 14 intents cannot have regressed.

4. Process topology — why three Python environments

Identical in voicebot-main and multilingual_ivr. merged_code 1 had one environment and one language.

|  MAINLINE NVIDIA NeMo      |  |  coqui-tts                      |

THE ISOLATION IS FORCED, NOT STYLISTIC:
  * IndicConformer (bn/hi) loads ONLY under AI4Bharat's NeMo fork;
    mainline raises  KeyError: 'dir'  on that checkpoint.
  * FastConformer (en) needs MAINLINE NeMo -- the two forks cannot both be
    importable in one interpreter. Hence English ASR is a 4th process.
  * coqui-tts needs huggingface_hub>=0.34; the AI4Bharat fork imports
    ModelFilter, REMOVED in 0.24. No single version satisfies both.
  => No version pair satisfies all three. Separate venvs, native processes,
     no Docker.

6. What the diagrams are really saying

The language layer is entirely voicebot-main's, untouched

LID-before-ASR, the two-decoder disambiguation for accented English, three routed engines, one TTS process for all three voices. The merge added nothing here and broke nothing here. merged_code 1 had none of it — it detected language from the Bengali transcript, which stops working the moment audio must be routed to one of three engines, because there is no transcript yet at the point the decision is needed.

The merge is a widening of Stage 5, not a redesign

Eight new guards sit in the deterministic chain ahead of the model. Two orderings are load-bearing and are asserted in tests rather than left as comments:

  • clinical before complaint — "I want to file a complaint, is my result dangerous" takes the clinical path; the health question must not go unanswered, and that path reaches a person too.
  • complaint before the request-for-a-person — a deliberate divergence from merged_code 1. Exactly one utterance collides: "I want to speak to someone about a complaint". Under the original order it is routed as a bare person-request and the complaint is never recorded — the call still looks fine, which is why it was worth changing.

merged_code 1's shape did not survive, deliberately

One 7,985-line main.py with a *_flow.py per story became detectors feeding voicebot-main's existing gate, policy and template machinery. Two concrete consequences:

  • Anger cost ~150 lines instead of 220. voicebot-main already contained a complete, trilingual "angry" speech policy and acknowledgement — and nothing ever set it. A grep returned three definition sites and nothing else. The feature existed, in three languages, and was unreachable. Only the detector was missing.
  • The doctor list cost ~150 lines instead of 423. The original carried its own department-name extractor — plural detection, Bengali case endings, fuzzy scoring with its own floor and margin. All of it was a text matcher in agent/ guessing at a name it had no list to check itself against. The new endpoint matches the caller's words against the real department table in both directions, so the clinic's own data decides and no extractor is needed.

Known limits, stated plainly

  • No Hindi or Bengali audio has gone through any of the new paths. The code paths are tested offline; the voices are not. Every Devanagari phrase list was written for this merge and needs a native speaker's review.
  • Department names are Latin-only in the schema, so a Bengali or Hindi reply names a department in a script that voice drops. Pre-existing — reply_templates.py:390 already does this — and the fix is localised department-name columns.
  • answer_ledger is code only. Wiring it needs a signature change across 12 call sites and 9 test files, which belongs in its own review.
  • date_calc was skipped. It would move calendar arithmetic out of the 7B model, which is a real truth-boundary win, but its design needs a new prompt slot (date_expr) and the prompt was out of scope.
